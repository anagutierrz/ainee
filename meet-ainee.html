Meet the Wedding Planner

Ainee Pimentel

Ainee Pimentel is a Dominican wedding planner and economist by profession, with a natural passion for thoughtful planning, refined details, and beautiful, curated celebrations.

Her journey began in the financial sector, working in administration before becoming a bride herself. While planning her own wedding, she discovered a genuine love for the world of weddings and the art of bringing every detail together with intention.

Today, Ainee combines her organizational expertise with a refined eye for design to create celebrations that feel effortless, personal, and beautifully curated.
